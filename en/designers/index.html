Beyond the box

ARCY has eleven architectural moves: twist, taper, lean, stack, cascade, cantilever, carve, slot, a diagonal court, a sloped or folded roof, and landform. Each is a parameter of the scheme, measured from its storeys and surfaces, and built into the IFC model.

A cantilever is a parameter, measured and built. Not a picture of one.

A bar to measure against

A reference library of 98 built works, each picture checked for its licence, chosen by building type, climate and quality. When ARCY critiques your scheme, the critic is shown that bar.

The character of the place

In Saudi Arabia, ARCY knows the 19 regional characters from the published design guidelines, each value with its page. Elsewhere, it looks up the rules of your place and cites where each one came from.

In its real surroundings

Your design can be shown among its real neighbours, from open data: footprints and heights from OpenStreetMap, the terrain and the real sun. A height the map lacks is estimated and drawn in grey. Nothing is invented.

Where it stands

Design quality is where we work hardest. In our real-user test, a single rater put mood boards at 7 for a client and 5 for a leading office, and interiors at 5 and 3, out of 10. The moves and the library came after that test, and are not yet measured.

The drawing stays yours

Every result is a file you open and edit. A change to your file comes as a proposal you accept or reject.
