A-310

The nineteen characters

ARCY works anywhere, and Saudi Arabia is where it goes deepest. These are the nineteen regional characters, each drawn from its published guideline.

Every place has its rules and its character. Here we know them value by value.

Page codes: G the design guidelines, V the villa handbook, L the low-rise handbook, p the page in the parameter file. Source: the Saudi architecture guidelines of the Architecture and Design Commission.

01 / 19

Drag, or use the arrow keys

  1. A-310.01

    النجدية الشمالية

    Northern Najdi

    Source

    • Opening ratio 1:2-1:7 G34; size 0.7-2.2 m p9
    • Parapet 0.4-1.6 m p9; five-step crenellations G10/G21
    • Ornament triangular fenestrations V9

    Not drawn

    • turmah (no drawn size)

  2. A-310.02

    ساحل تبوك

    Tabuk Coast

    Source

    • Opening villa window 0.9 x 2 m and vent 0.4 x 0.45 m V10
    • Parapet 0.7-1.8 m p10; flat horizontal parapets G22

    Not drawn

    • roshan (higher-status buildings only)

  3. A-310.03

    المدينة المنورة

    Inner Madinah

    Source

    • Roshan mashrabiya/roshan 1.9-3.7 m p10; roshans the most important feature V3

    Not drawn

    • window proportion (the books disagree, p26)
    • parapet height (tall, no number)

    ريف المدينة المنورة

    Madinah Rural

    Source

    • Opening traditional windows 1.0-1.2 x 1.1-1.4 m G31; expressed lintels G43
    • Ornament 0.6 m triangle vent V10

    Not drawn

    • parapet (stepped roof profile, no height)

  5. A-310.05

    النجدية

    Central Najdi

    Source

    • Opening ratio 1:2-1:5 G38; size 0.8-2.4 m p9
    • Parapet 0.3-0.8 m p9; five-step crenellations G25
    • Ornament triangular bands G11/G25

  6. A-310.06

    النجدية الشرقية

    Eastern Najdi

    Source

  7. A-310.07

    Qatif Oasis

    Source

    • Parapet 0.5-1.8 m, typical 1.1 m p5

    Not drawn

  8. A-310.08

    الساحل الشرقي

    East Coast

    Source

    • Opening ratio 1:1.5-1:3 p34; semicircular arch in frame V5
    • Parapet 0.7-1.7 m, typical 1.3 m p9; mostly no cornice G21

  9. A-310.09

    الأحساء

    Al-Ahsa Oasis

    Not drawn

    • lotus-cusp arch ornament (not dimensioned)

  10. A-310.10

    الحجازية الساحلية

    Hejazi Coast

    Source

    • Roshan roshan 3.7 x 4.1 m projecting 0.9 m V10
    • Parapet 0.8-2.0 m, typical 2.0 m p10

  11. A-310.11

    الطائف

    Taif Highlands

    Source

    • Opening arch-top window 1.2 x 3.0 m V10
    • Parapet 1.0-1.2 m p10; flat parapets with circular openings G24

  12. A-310.12

    بيشة الصحراوية

    Bisha Desert

    Source

    • Opening ratio 1:1.3-1:2 G31; white bands V5
    • Parapet 0.6 m read from figures p31; articulated parapets G21
    • Wall batter 3° batter, within the guidelines' 5° limit

    Not drawn

    • mud-brick courses (spacing not given)

  13. A-310.13

    جبال السروات

    Sarawat Mountains

    Source

    • Opening ratio 1:1.5-1:2 p5; heavy stone head and sill G31
    • Parapet 1.0-1.5 m read from figures p31
    • Ornament triangular stone patterns G43

    Not drawn

    • Al Sadu motifs (pattern not specified)

    مرتفعات أبها

    Abha Highlands

    Source

    • Opening ratio 1:1.2-1:2 G33; white surrounds V5
    • Ornament Al-Ragaf slate courses G22 (spacing illustrative)
    • Wall batter 3° batter, within the guidelines' 5° limit

    Not drawn

    • parapet height (no number)

  15. A-310.15

    أصدار عسير

    Aseer Escarpment

    Source

    • Opening ratio 1:1-1:1.5 p31; white-framed windows G11
    • Ornament band of square motifs above V10
    • Wall batter 3° batter, within the guidelines' 5° limit

    Not drawn

    • stone parapet height (no number)

  16. A-310.16

    نجران

    Najran

    Source

    • Opening window typical 1.0 m p5; small square above V5/L5
    • Parapet 1.0-3.3 m, typical 1.6 m p9; raised corners, crowned parapets G21
    • Ornament horizontal mud bands (Midmakh) G8/G20 (spacing illustrative)
    • Wall batter 3° batter, within the guidelines' 5° limit

    Source

    Not drawn
