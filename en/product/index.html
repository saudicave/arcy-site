What sets ARCY apart

What sets ARCY apart

  1. 01

    Its own guides and engines

    It works from 34 guides of its own and 71 capabilities. Its numbers engines are held to worked examples, among them a development appraisal with NPV, IRR, VAT, real-estate transaction tax and the white-land fee.

  2. 02

    Inside your design programs, through one bridge

    It runs today inside Blender, where each change is a proposal applied as one undoable step. Connectors for Rhino, Revit, AutoCAD and GIS are specified, not yet written.

  3. 03

    Sun, daylight and energy, held to published references

    Sun position is checked against NREL SPA, daylight against CIE 171 and Radiance, within 3% of it per room, and energy against ASHRAE 140.

  4. 04

    Checked before it reaches you

    An independent judge reviews the work before delivery. Drawings, model, quantities and feasibility are held to one another, and a mismatch goes back with both numbers.

  5. 05

    It researches in the open

    Its own search engine first, then scholarly indexes, and a browser you can watch. Its critic is shown the bar: a library of 98 built works of award level, with licence-checked pictures.

  6. 06

    Plans reviewed against sourced rules

    Subdivision plans are checked against 93 rules, each with its document and page; 12 are not yet verified. Every Saudi rule carries its source, edition and last check, and stale ones are flagged. A draft review, not an approval.

  7. 07

    It asks only what it needs

    Before substantial work it asks a few questions, each with a default, in views built from twelve trusted components. Each preference it remembers shows where it came from, with a switch to turn it off.

  8. 08

    Sub-agents in parallel

    Up to four at once, each with its own budget and colour, their outputs kept apart.

  9. 09

    Beyond the box

    Eleven architectural moves, among them twist, taper, lean, stack, cascade and cantilever, are parameters of a scheme, measured and built into IFC. Free-form surfaces go out to Rhino and STEP.

  10. 10

    The nineteen Saudi regional characters

    Each held in its data, value by value, with the page of the guideline it came from.
