Privacy modes

Two modes are planned for work that must not leave a closed setting: protected and sovereign. For those two modes only, ARCY would run on open-weight models on a dedicated server. Hosting inside the Kingdom does not exist yet and is not announced before it does.

Permit-ready packages

A set of drawings and documents prepared in the form a permit submission asks for. Coming.

The job queue

When the server is busy with heavy work, your run will show its place in line, quietly beside its clock. Built, not yet merged.
