For investors

Who we are, what we are building, what we have measured, and how to reach us. There are no financial figures on this page.

This page is information about the company. It is not an offer to sell securities or a solicitation to invest, and nothing on it promises a return. Draft wording, not yet reviewed by a lawyer.

Who we are

ARCY was born in Saudi Arabia. The idea came about two years ago, in the field, and was built beside a full-time job, through hard years and fifteen failed attempts. Results began about four months ago. We are launching late, on purpose, for quality. The whole account is on the About page.

What we are building

One agent for architecture, design, engineering and urban planning. It works with a person on one shared canvas, runs its own engines, and delivers files the person opens and edits. What is built today, with the record behind each claim, is on the Product page.

Who it is for

Offices, sovereign entities, real-estate developers, and professionals and designers. ARCY works on any project, anywhere. Saudi Arabia is where it goes deepest: its regulations, its 19 regional characters, and plan review.

Why now

An agent can now do what a chat cannot: run engines, draw, model, check a plan against its rules, and show every step. The work an architect most needs checked can be measured, and we publish what we measure.

The work an architect most needs checked can be measured. So we measure it, and publish it.

What is measured so far

On 23 November 2026 the agent ran 19 paid tasks and 11 passed. The owner's verdict put no output above 1 of 4; 3 is what a large consultancy would show a client. After the first fixes, 3 of 4 repaired tasks pass, and the drawing set rose from 2 to 2.8 of 4.

A day later, a real-user test sent 27 requests through 17 sessions, in Saudi dialect, formal Arabic and English. No request was misread, dialect and typos included. 22.8% of the agent's steps failed, mostly from two avoidable errors. Every number, with its file, is on the results page. We show no outputs until they reach the target.

The road ahead

A fixed sequence. Each step ends on a mark, not on a date.

  1. The outputs: a complete villa journey at 3 of 4 from the owner on every output.
  2. Subdivision plan review: a review of a real plan that the owner accepts.
  3. The interface, rebuilt: the first five minutes succeed without explanation.
  4. Reliability and speed: the ten most common requests succeed 9 of 10 times.
  5. A final review of everything, then a real cloud deployment with payment, backup and monitoring.
  6. The announcement, last, when all of it is accepted.

There are no dates because the plan has none.
