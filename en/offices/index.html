  1. A-202

    For offices

    An office comes with a project that needs a drawing set, a model, a review before submission and a schedule of quantities.

  2. A-202.2

    A drawing set that agrees with itself.

    Plan, section and schedule come from one model, so a change in one is a change in all of them.

  3. A-202.3

    A review before submission.

    The set is read against the regulations, and each judgement names its clause and its page.

  4. A-202.4

    Quantities from the same model.

    The schedule is counted from what was drawn, not typed beside it.

Abstract drawing, not an ARCY output.

What an office needs

A consistent drawing set, a model to build on, a review against the regulations before submission, and quantities from the same model.

Your projects stay yours

An office's projects are its own: every route in the product is scoped to the account holder.
