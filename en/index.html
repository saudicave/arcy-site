    An architectural mind that works with you

    One conversation, one shared canvas. It draws, builds the model and checks the regulations, and every result is a file you can edit.

    Scroll slowly: the drawing moves one stage at a time.

  2. A-101.2

    The line becomes a measured plan.

    Walls take their thickness and rooms their dimensions. Point at a room: its area is computed from the geometry.

  3. A-101.3

    ARCY works where you can see it.

    Its named cursor comes onto the shared canvas, marks the region it is working in and draws there, while its plan ticks itself: done, now, next.

  4. A-101.4

    The plan rises into a building.

    Storey by storey, up to the parapet. Turn it, and move the sun over Riyadh: the shadows are computed as you go.

    Drag the drawing to turn the house; move the sun with the slider.

  5. A-101.5

    The building opens to be read.

    A section cuts it where you point, and what it cuts fills black.

    Move the pointer over the drawing, or the slider, to move the section.

    Any city, anywhere: ARCY reads its rules and reviews the plan against them. Here the review mark lands on an inner plot with no street frontage.

Saudi by origin and specialty. For anywhere.

ARCY works on any project, anywhere, with the rules and character of its place. Saudi architecture is where we are deepest: its nineteen regional characters are in its data, value by value and source by source.

What sets ARCY apart

Ten things that are built today, each with the record it rests on. How good the outputs are is a separate question, answered on the results page.

  1. 01

    An agent with its own guides and engines

    It reads 34 guides of its own and calls 71 capabilities: engines for drawing, modelling, daylight, energy, quantities, review and more.

  2. 02

    Inside design programs, through one bridge

    Running today inside Blender, where every change is a proposal applied as one undoable step. Connectors for Rhino, Revit, AutoCAD and GIS are specified and coming; they are not written yet.

  3. 03

    A daylight engine held to an independent solution

    At the seven points checked it is within 3% of an independent radiosity solution. The comparison with Radiance has not been rerun yet.

  4. 04

    It checks its own work before it says done

    A sheet is read back against the model it was drawn from, and an answer may not call an output finished over a check that failed or was not run.

  5. 05

    It researches in the open

    Its own search engine first, then scholarly indexes, and a browser it drives where you can watch every step. Only pages it has opened are cited.

  6. 06

    Subdivision plans, reviewed against sourced rules

    A register of 93 rules, each with its document and page; 12 of them are not yet verified. What it gives is a draft review, not an approval.

  7. 07

    It composes its interface for the moment

    Views assembled from twelve trusted components: choices, a slider, a comparison, a pointer at the thing it asks about.

  8. 08

    Sub-agents in parallel

    Up to four at once, each with its own budget and its own colour, their outputs kept apart.

  9. 09

    Free-form surfaces, to IFC and Rhino

    Parametric surfaces, fields and panels, the shell written into the IFC and exported to Rhino and STEP files. Its two evidence designs are not made yet.

  10. 10

    The nineteen Saudi regional characters

    Each one in its data, value by value, with the page of the guideline it came from.

Abstract drawings, not ARCY outputs and not pictures of its interface. The numbers come from the product record of 24 November 2026.
