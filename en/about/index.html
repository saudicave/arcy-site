  1. A-301

    About ARCY

    A Saudi company building one agent for architecture, design, engineering and urban planning. This is how it came to be.

  2. A-301.2

    It began in the field.

    About two years ago, on site, the idea came: a mind that draws, reviews and revises with the architect. We began to build it beside a full-time job, in the hours that were left.

  3. A-301.3

    Then came the hard years.

    The work was technically hard. There was no funding, and the idea was not taken seriously. There was an attempt to take the idea from us; we answered by continuing to build. And always the pressure of the job, of the team, and of time that was never enough.

  4. A-301.4

    Fifteen attempts failed.

    Each was begun in earnest, and each fell short. We kept what it had taught us and began again.

  5. A-301.5

    About four months ago, results began to come.

    We do not ask to be believed. On 23 November 2026 we ran 19 paid tasks and 11 passed; the owner's verdict put no output above 1 of 4. After the first fixes, 3 of 4 repaired tasks pass, and the drawing set has risen from 2 to 2.8 of 4.

  6. A-301.6

    We are launching late, on purpose.

    We could have opened earlier, with less. We open when a complete villa journey scores 3 of 4 on every output, and not before.

A drawn timeline of our own account. Its measured part comes from the result files.

We chose to arrive late, with work we can stand behind.

Why

Architecture is long work of drawing, reviewing and revising. We want an agent that works with the architect through all of it, shows its work as real files, and is measured.

How we work

We measure before we claim, and publish what we found, weak results included, on the results page.

Where we are deepest

ARCY works anywhere, with the rules and the character of the place. Saudi architecture is where it is deepest: the nineteen regional characters are in its data, value by value and source by source.
