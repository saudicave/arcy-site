For

Four readers, each with a real task. Here is what ARCY does for each today, as measured, and what it does not do yet.

  • A-202For officesAn office comes with a project that needs a drawing set, a model, a review before submission and a schedule of quantities.
  • A-203For sovereign entitiesA sovereign or government entity comes with plans and projects to review against its regulations, and needs every judgement traced to its source.
  • A-204For real-estate developersA developer comes with a plot and questions: what can be built on it, how much floor area, at what cost, and what surrounds it.
  • A-205For professionals and designersA designer comes with a sketch or an idea, and wants it to become a plan, a model and pictures to show a client.
