  1. A-204

    For real-estate developers

    A developer comes with a plot and questions: what can be built on it, how much floor area, at what cost, and what surrounds it.

  2. A-204.2

    First, what the rules leave you.

    The setbacks are taken off the plot, and what remains is the ground you can build on.

  3. A-204.3

    Then, what can rise on it.

    A first massing, storey by storey, drawn on the plot it belongs to.

  4. A-204.4

    And figures you can trace.

    Footprint and floor area are computed from the geometry that is drawn. The example is abstract, not a planning rule.

Abstract drawing, not an ARCY output.

What a developer needs

A quick reading of the plot, its rules and its surroundings, a first massing with its areas, and figures that can be traced.

What ARCY does not do

ARCY gives no buying advice: it records and prices what it is given, and the decision is the developer's.
