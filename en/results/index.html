The method

A suite of paid tasks in which the agent works as it would with a real person; each criterion is then measured from the files it delivered, not from what it says. A task pass means a capability was used correctly; output quality is judged by the owner on a four-step scale, where 3 is what a large consultancy would show a client.

Each new measurement is added with its date, and the old ones are never removed.

The owner's verdict on quality

The owner's verdict after looking at every output: none above 1 of 4, on a scale where 3 is what a large consultancy would show a client. A pass in this suite says a capability was used correctly, not that its output is good.

2026-11-23

The limits of these numbers

One run per task and a small number: four tasks after the fixes say a direction and no more (McNemar p = 0.25). Times were taken on a loaded machine and are upper bounds. Research, memory, project records and others were not measured. Task c40 still fails.
