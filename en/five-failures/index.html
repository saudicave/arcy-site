E-102

Five failures, and none was repaired in the model

Draft article: not published or announced; it waits for the guard's and the owner's verdict.

Each time our agent failed, the repair was in something we had built around the model: a tool, a record, an answer, a guide, never a new instruction. A second model taught us the same lesson within hours.

After measuring ARCY on nineteen paid tasks, we read the records of each failure step by step. The quick repair is one more sentence in the agent's instructions. Our plan rules that out as a first step: the tool, the guide or the error message is repaired before any instruction is added.

1. An empty field read as a request

The task: the window ratio from a regional design guideline, with its page. The value is in the data. The agent looked for it 31 times and never got it.

The lookup tool has an optional part for assessing a value. With a plain question, the model also sent that part, with an empty name and zeros. The tool read every call as an assessment, and answered that the parameter was not found.

The repair is in the tool. An assessment now needs a parameter name, and a name the tool does not know is answered with the ones it may mean. The task then passed in 1 step and 11 seconds; before, it had failed after 33 steps and 104 seconds.

2. A record that refused what a real sketch leaves blank

A plot sketch does not show everything: the municipality, the neighbours, the width of a street that is not there. For each missing figure the model sent an empty text or a zero; the plot record refused them as invalid, and after four refusals the capability closed for the request.

Now a figure filled with nothing is left out as not read. A zero that points at a real place on the page is still a misread, and a side still needs its length. The task went from 7 steps with 5 failed to 2 steps with none failed.

The first two share one root, and it is on record.

A model held to a strict schema fills every field it does not mean.

Sources

  • data/RESULTS-section-20.md

3. A listing with no link

A person typed two plots with their prices and asked for a comparison. The property study kept a listing only with its web address, a rule made for provenance. Both typed listings were refused.

Now such a listing is kept, recorded as said by the person, when its price and area are in their own words. Figures they never said are still refused. After the repair both were kept, each at the person's price per square metre.

4. Links that open nothing

The model linked its files by their path inside the sandbox, a habit from another product, so the person saw link text that opened nothing. This happened in 4 of 8 answers of one batch. The answer now carries the file's name.

  • data/RESULTS-section-20.md

5. Guides that pointed the wrong way

First guide: asked for a site of four plots, the agent read the one-plot guide, drew a plan with code and never made the site model, which the several-plots guide never mentioned. We corrected both. This repair has not worked yet. In the rerun the right guide was read; the site model was still not made.

Second guide, found in the next cycle: the drawing guide named the one-call drawing set only in its fifth point, so the agent drew every sheet itself and delivered a plan with two overall dimensions. The set draws grids, dimension chains, section marks and a north arrow; the guide now names it first. This repair has no before-and-after figure. The owner's verdicts on the set are 2 of 4 before its own repairs (the agent's sheet: 1 or less) and 2.8 after two rounds. Still below the bar of 3.

  • evidence/plan-2026-11-24-extract.md

How much the repairs moved

We reran the four tasks the first five repairs touch. 3 of 4 pass now; 0 of 4 did before. Four tasks with one run each give a direction and no more (p = 0.25). The three that passed were repairs to a tool or a record. The one that still fails is the guide.

  • data/RESULTS-section-20.md

The same lesson from a second model

In ARCY the model is the person's choice, so the agent must work with several. On 24 November we tried a second provider's thinking model on 8 tasks. In 6 of 8 the provider refused the request right after a tool call.

The provider requires the model's reasoning to come back with the tool calls it led to, and our adapter dropped it. The first repair sent it back, never shown to the person. That was not enough: when the model spoke and called tools in one turn, words and calls went as two messages, and the refusal returned. The second repair made them one.

In the next run, on 9 tasks, none was refused. 6 of 9 passed, against 4 of 8 before (the second run added one task). One of the three failures ended with an unfinished answer, a different fault, still open. This says the refusal is gone. It does not rank the two models.

Again the repair was on our side of the conversation, not in the model.

Sources

  • evidence/second-model-trial.json

The principle

An instruction is written for the model in front of you. A tool that reads what was meant, a record that accepts what people really give, and an adapter that returns what a provider needs serve every model that may come after it.

What we do next

  • The site of four plots still fails and stays on the list.
  • Two gaps are open: a review of a person's own document cannot yet be re-checked by measurement, and the facade engine has no shading devices. Two failed tasks were not touched.
  • The plan's mark for this first cycle is that at least 6 of the 8 failed tasks pass when run again. Four have been rerun and three pass.
