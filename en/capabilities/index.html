Capabilities

What ARCY does today. Only what has landed in the product is on this page.

Design quality

  • A bar to aim at. A library of 98 built works of award level, each picture licence-checked. The critic is shown that bar before it judges.
  • An independent judge before delivery. Options are first held to conditions: shade, outdoor room, daylight, programme. Among those that meet them, an independent judge orders them as architecture, and its order decides what goes forward.
  • Eleven architectural moves. Twist, taper, lean, stack, cascade, cantilever, carve, slot, a diagonal court, a sloped or folded roof, and landform. Each is a parameter of the scheme, measured and built into the IFC model.
  • Made by hand, not by machine. A written standard we hold: every output, charts and heat maps included, passes through crafted styles. It is a standard, not a measurement.

Among the options that meet the conditions, the judge's order leads.

Engines

  • Environment. Sun position, daylight, solar radiation and energy, each checked against a published reference: NREL SPA, CIE 171, Radiance and ASHRAE 140.
  • Numbers. Structure, services and cost engines checked against worked examples.
  • A development appraisal. NPV, IRR, VAT, real-estate transaction tax and the white-land fee, each figure with its unit, method and source.

Trust

  • One story across the outputs. Drawings, model, quantities and feasibility are checked against each other before delivery. A mismatch goes back with both numbers and their sources.
  • Every rule with its source. Each Saudi rule carries its document, edition and the date it was last checked. A stale or undated rule is flagged.
  • What it reads is not an order. Outside material is fenced as data, hidden text is removed and reported, and nothing it reads can make it send or keep anything. More on security.
  • A hard cost cap. Before every model call, ARCY checks the call fits in what is left. If it does not, the call is not made.

Context

The design is placed in its real surroundings from its coordinates: buildings, streets and ground from OpenStreetMap and other open data, each source named with its date and licence. A height the data lacks is estimated and drawn as estimated. Nothing is invented.

Agent

  • It remembers what you prefer. Each preference shows where it came from, and has a switch to turn it off.
  • A few questions first, when they matter. Before substantial work it asks a few questions, each with a default. A complete brief goes straight to work.
  • The character of every region. It knows the regional character of Saudi Arabia's 19 regions, value by value. See the characters.

Studio

  • The new Studio. One conversation and one canvas. When a run ends, the answer comes first, with its review folded under it.
  • Views the agent composes. When a choice or a table says it better than words, the agent builds the view from a fixed set of trusted components. Your choice comes back to it as part of the work.
