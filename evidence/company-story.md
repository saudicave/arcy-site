# The company's story: the account the About page tells

The About page and the home page's short echo tell how ARCY came to be. That story is the company's own account,
given by its founder on 2026-11-24 and recorded here as given. It is testimony, not a measurement: nothing in it
was counted by a tool, and the site says so on the drawing ("a drawn timeline of our own account"). No names are
given, by the founder's choice.

The account, and nothing beyond it, is what the page may say:

- The idea came about two years ago, while the founder was working in the field.
- ARCY was built beside a full-time job.
- The hard years: technical difficulty; no funding and not being taken seriously; an attempt to take the idea;
  the pressure of the job; the team and the lack of time.
- Fifteen attempts failed.
- About four months ago results started to come.
- The launch is late on purpose, for quality.
